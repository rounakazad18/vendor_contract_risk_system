# VENDOR CLOUD SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Cloud Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of November 15, 2026 ("Effective Date"), by and between GlobalVendor SaaS Technologies Inc. ("Vendor"), and Customer Enterprise Corp. ("Customer").

1. FEES AND AUTOMATIC PRICE ESCALATION
Customer agrees to pay all invoiced subscription fees within fifteen (15) calendar days from receipt of invoice. Late payments shall incur interest at the rate of three percent (3.0%) per month or the maximum statutory rate permitted by law. Vendor reserves the unilateral right to increase subscription fees annually by fifteen percent (15%) upon fifteen (15) days' electronic notice to Customer. Customer may not contest or audit any invoiced charges.

2. SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT AND DOWNTIME EXCLUSIONS
Vendor shall endeavor to maintain ninety-two percent (92.0%) uptime across calendar quarters, excluding scheduled maintenance, emergency maintenance, infrastructure updates, and third-party hosting failures. Customer sole and exclusive remedy for any downtime or service outage shall be a service credit capped at five percent (5%) of the monthly recurring fee for the affected service component. Under no circumstances may Customer terminate this Agreement for prolonged service unavailability or recurring outages.

3. DATA OWNERSHIP AND TRAINING DERIVATIVE RIGHTS
Customer retains underlying ownership of Customer Data provided to the platform. Notwithstanding the foregoing, Vendor is hereby granted an irrevocable, perpetual, royalty-free, worldwide, transferable license to aggregate, anonymize, extract, decompile, and process Customer Data and telemetry to train, tune, validate, and commercialize Vendor's proprietary artificial intelligence models, machine learning algorithms, and commercial benchmark analytics.

4. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION AND SECURITY
In the event of a confirmed security incident or unauthorized exfiltration of Customer sensitive personal data, Vendor shall make commercially reasonable efforts to notify Customer within thirty (30) business days following completion of Vendor's internal forensic investigation. Vendor disclaims any responsibility for third-party subcontractor security breaches or cloud infrastructure compromises.

5. TOTAL LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, IN NO EVENT SHALL VENDOR'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT (INCLUDING NEGLIGENCE), INDEMNITY, BREACH OF CONFIDENTIALITY, OR GROSS NEGLIGENCE, EXCEED FIVE HUNDRED UNITED STATES DOLLARS ($500.00) OR THE AMOUNT ACTUALLY PAID BY CUSTOMER IN THE PRECEDING ONE (1) MONTH. VENDOR EXPRESSLY DISCLAIMS ALL LIABILITY FOR CONSEQUENTIAL, INDIRECT, SPECIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

6. UNILATERAL INDEMNIFICATION BY CUSTOMER
Customer shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Vendor, its officers, directors, employees, affiliates, and suppliers against any third-party claims, regulatory enforcement actions, fines, judgments, damages, and legal costs arising out of Customer's use of the services, alleged data protection violations, or Customer Data uploaded to Vendor servers. Vendor provides no intellectual property infringement indemnification to Customer whatsoever.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY AND PUBLICITY
Each party agrees to safeguard Confidential Information with reasonable care. However, Customer's obligations under this Section shall survive indefinitely, whereas Vendor's confidentiality obligations shall expire six (6) months following the termination of this Agreement. Vendor retains the absolute right to issue press releases, post marketing announcements, and utilize Customer's corporate name, logo, and trademark on Vendor's public website and marketing brochures without prior consent.

8. TERMINATION AND DATA RETURN
This Agreement commences on the Effective Date for an initial term of thirty-six (36) months and shall automatically renew for successive twenty-four (24) month terms unless Customer gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. Customer shall have no right of termination for convenience. Upon termination for any reason, Vendor may immediately delete all Customer Data and backups within forty-eight (48) hours unless Customer pays a data extraction fee of $15,000 within twenty-four (24) hours of termination.

9. AUDIT RIGHTS AND ACCESS
Customer shall possess no right to audit Vendor facilities, security controls, SOC2 reports, penetration test summaries, or financial books and records under any circumstances. Vendor may, upon forty-eight (48) hours' notice, remotely inspect or audit Customer's computer systems, networks, and usage logs to verify compliance with license limits.

10. GOVERNING LAW AND VENUE
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Cayman Islands, without regard to conflict of laws principles. The parties irrevocably consent to the exclusive jurisdiction and venue of the courts of Grand Cayman, Cayman Islands, for any dispute, controversy, or claim arising hereunder. Customer waives all rights to a jury trial or class action participation.
